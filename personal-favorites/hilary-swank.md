# Hilary Swank

## 🎤 Personal Statement
"Part of my life was growing up in Grays Harbor, Aberdeen, Washington, and Hoquiam, Washington. My grandfather owned 2000 Broadway Villa in Woodlawn out there, and a couple of trailers down was an individual named Jennifer Patton. She announced that her cousin is Hilary Swank, who she has had contact with, which totally surprised me when she shared photos with me and my cousin at a young age. We ended up watching her for the first time at that trailer, and I've enjoyed her performances ever since."
