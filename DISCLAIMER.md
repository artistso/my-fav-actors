# Objectivity and Authorship Disclaimer

This archive combines **externally verifiable facts** with the author's **first-person recollections, interpretations, rankings, and subjective opinions**.

The author is 42 years old and describes their experience as that of **a rare hyperthymestic mind**. Statements labeled **Personal account**, **Personal recollection**, or **Opinion** are the author's factual account of what they remember or personally experienced, but they are presented as first-person testimony rather than independent clinical, historical, or scientific proof.

Statements labeled **Verified fact** concern externally checkable film, television, biographical, or production information and should include a source. A factual correction supported by a reliable source is welcome and should not be treated as a challenge to the author's personal memory or subjective assessment.

This archive therefore uses three explicit classes:

1. **Verified fact** - externally checkable and source-supported.
2. **Personal account** - what the author remembers experiencing or being told.
3. **Opinion** - the author's aesthetic judgment, interpretation, ranking, or preference.

Private individuals, precise residential locations, and other unnecessary identifying details are intentionally generalized unless publication is clearly justified and consented to.
