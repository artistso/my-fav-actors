# John Candy

## 🎤 Personal Statement
"The first time that I became aware of John Candy was in his performance of Uncle Buck and Planes, Trains, and Automobiles. Leading me to check out his past performances as well and becoming a fan. And while I have heard from a lot of people comparing him to Chris Farley, saying that Chris Farley takes from John Candy's performance, I see them as separate, unique individuals. They're just focusing on body shape and not the actual history and performance. The two of them give off completely two different performances, even though Chris Farley, I think, was a fan of John Candy, as most people were and still are today."

## Notable Works
- **SCTV (1976–1984)**: Multiple iconic characters
- **Splash (1984)**: Freddie Bauer
- **Planes, Trains and Automobiles (1987)**: Del Griffith (career-defining)
- **The Great Outdoors (1988)**: Chet Ripley
- **Uncle Buck (1989)**: Russell / Uncle Buck
- **Cool Runnings (1993)**: Irv Blitzer
- **Wagons East (1994)**: James Harlow (final performance)
- **Canadian Bacon (1995)**: Bud Boomer (posthumous release)
