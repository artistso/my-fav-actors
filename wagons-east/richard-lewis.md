# Richard Lewis

## 🎤 Personal Statement
"I love that in this film, Richard Lewis is called up to give a stand-up performance halfway through the film to his wagon companions as a means of relaxation and restoration to their humanity. He describes a story about him being a surgeon during the war and an individual who took a cannonball to the chest. He did his best, and he saved his life up until the point where he realized he left a bologna sandwich in there where his lungs should be. The reason why it was so funny is that during his standup routine, they're so short. He was describing how he worked tirelessly day after day, pulling shift after shift, eating what he could, when he could, thus revealing that he was eating during the surgery. Instead of completely fixing up the individual that he was attempting to repair, you left a baloney sandwich in there, and it was hilarious. It's so deadpan. It's so Richard Lewis and his real-life standup comedy."

## 📺 The First Recall (DVR Context)
"The first time that I had seen it I was in Las Vegas, Nevada, at my second stepfather's apartment up at the lakes, living in Summerhill Point Apartments. He had one of those black boxes from Cox Communications. The installer tweaked it out so that he got every free channel, and we had HBO, Show Time, and more."
