# John C. McGinley

## 🎤 Personal Statement
"I'm currently watching John C. McGinley in Wagons East yet once again, and his performance is brilliant as a gay individual. His personality in life seems to reflect this mannerism and tonality, no matter, realistically, what he's doing. It comes off as such dry, witty humor while also being incredibly smiling with his perfect teeth and his well-structured performance. I like that in the beginning of the film, he portrays himself as a very high-end, sophisticated, philosophical literist, selling books, wearing riches, and a nice, good pink bow tie that I'd really love to have. Then, halfway during the film, when one thinks that all the characters are at their most desperate and doomed, John C. McGinley's character comes out of nowhere as the most professional shooter and saves the day!"
