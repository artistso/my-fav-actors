# The Adventures of Milo and Otis

## Ranking

**Absolute favorite movie of all time.**

## Verified facts

- The official story centers on Milo, a curious kitten, and Otis, a pug-nosed puppy. Source: [Sony Pictures Entertainment](https://www.sonypictures.com/movies/theadventuresofmiloandotis).
- Dudley Moore is credited as narrator of the U.S. version. Source: [Rotten Tomatoes cast and crew](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/the_adventures_of_milo_and_otis_1986/cast-and-crew).

## Personal account and opinion

My absolute favorite movie of all time is *The Adventures of Milo and Otis*. Its visual world is centered on animals rather than visible human characters. Although the U.S. version includes narration by Dudley Moore, I can mute the narration and still follow and enjoy the film without visual distraction.

When working from a personal-use copy that I am legally permitted to edit, I also enjoy reducing or removing the vocal narration while retaining the environmental sounds. This creates a calm, animal-centered viewing experience that is especially wonderful to share with my cats.

The ability to experience the story visually, without requiring human figures or spoken narration, is the central reason it ranks above every other film for me.
