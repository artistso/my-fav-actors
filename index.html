The Adventures of Milo and Otis

My absolute favorite movie of all time is The Adventures of Milo and Otis. Its visual world is centered on animals rather than visible human characters. Although the U.S. version includes narration by Dudley Moore, I can mute the narration and still follow and enjoy the film without visual distraction.

Retaining the environmental sounds while reducing the vocal narration creates an especially calm viewing experience to share with my cats.
